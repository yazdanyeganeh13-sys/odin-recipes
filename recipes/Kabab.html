Kabab Koobideh

Kabāb koobideh is an Iranian kabāb made from ground lamb, often mixed with salt, ground black pepper and grated onions

Ingredients

  • Meat: Ground beef (80/20 fat ratio) and ground lamb (mixed together for best flavor and fat content)
  • Aromatics: Yellow onion (grated, with all liquid squeezed out)
  • Spices & Seasoning:Ground sumac,Turmeric,Salt and black pepper
  • Color & Flavor: Ground saffron (bloomed in hot water)
  • Optional Binding: Baking soda (a tiny pinch—helps keep the meat tender and attached to the skewer)

Steps

  1. Grate & Drain the Onion: Grate the yellow onion using a fine grater. Squeeze out as much onion juice as possible using a cheesecloth or fine mesh strainer. (Discard the juice or save it for a marinade—only the dry onion pulp goes into the meat so it doesn't fall off the skewers!)
  2. Knead the Meat Mixture: In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, ground lamb, squeezed onion pulp, salt, black pepper, sumac, turmeric, and half of the bloomed saffron water. Knead the mixture by hand for 10-15 minutes until it becomes sticky, paste-like, and smooth.
  3. Chill the Meat: Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 1 to 2 hours. Chilling helps the fat solidify so the meat stays firmly on the skewers.
  4. Skewering (Koobideh Style): Wet your hands with cold water. Take a fist-sized ball of meat, press it onto a wide flat metal skewer, and spread it evenly down the skewer. Use your thumb and forefinger to make distinct indentations along the length of the kabab.
  5. Grill the Kababs: Cook over high heat on a hot charcoal grill (or gas grill). Turn the skewers frequently during the first 2–3 minutes so the meat sears evenly and doesn't slide off. Grill for 6–8 minutes total until fully cooked and browned.
  6. Baste & Serve: Brush the kababs with melted butter mixed with the remaining saffron water right as they come off the grill. Serve hot on top of flatbread (like Sangak or Lavash) alongside saffron rice, grilled tomatoes, and extra sumac.
