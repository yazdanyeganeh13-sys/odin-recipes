Makaroni

Makaroni is the popular Iranian version of spaghetti or pasta, cooked with a savory meat and tomato sauce and then steamed to create a crispy, golden bottom crust called tahdig

Ingredients

  • Pasta: 1 lb spaghetti (thicker spaghetti, around #3 or #4, works best)
  • Meat & Vegetables:1 lb ground beef or lamb,1 large yellow onion, finely diced,1 cup white button mushrooms, sliced (optional),1/2 green bell pepper, finely diced (optional)
  • Sauce & Seasoning:3–4 tbsp tomato paste,1 tsp ground turmeric,1/2 tsp cinnamon (the secret Persian ingredient!),1 tbsp bloomed saffron liquid (optional, but adds great aroma),Salt and black pepper to taste,Cooking oil and butter
  • For the Tahdig (Crispy Bottom Layer):2 medium potatoes, sliced into 1/4-inch rounds (or extra lavash/flatbread)

Steps

  1. Boil the Pasta: Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti until it is slightly undercooked (about 2–3 minutes less than the package instructions for al dente, as it will finish cooking while steaming). Drain and rinse briefly with cold water to stop the cooking.
  2. Make the Meat Sauce: Heat oil in a pan and saute the diced onions until golden. Add the ground beef, turmeric, salt, black pepper, and cinnamon. Cook until browned.
  3. Add Vegetables & Tomato Paste: Stir in the green bell pepper and mushrooms if using. Add the tomato paste and fry it in the oil for 2 minutes until it turns dark red and fragrant. Add 1/4 cup of warm water, mix well, and simmer for 5 minutes until thick.
  4. Prepare the Potato Tahdig: Wash and dry the main pot. Add 2–3 tablespoons of oil and a pinch of turmeric (and saffron water if using) to the bottom of the pot. Arrange the potato slices in a single layer to cover the entire bottom.
  5. Layer & Steam: Layer a portion of cooked spaghetti over the potatoes, followed by a layer of meat sauce. Repeat the layers, building a pyramid/mound shape. Poke 3 or 4 holes through the pasta mound down to the bottom with the handle of a wooden spoon to let steam escape.
  6. Steam (Dam-kash): Cover the lid with a clean kitchen towel (to catch moisture) and seal tightly on the pot. Cook on medium heat for 5 minutes until steam builds up, then turn the heat to low and let it steam for 35–45 minutes until the potatoes turn into golden, crispy Tahdig.
