Tahchin

Tahchin or tahcheen is an Iranian rice dish primarily consisting of rice, yogurt, eggs, saffron, and various types of meat. Tahchin translates from Persian as "arranged on the bottom"

Ingredients

  • Rice & Batter: 3 cups Basmati rice, 1.5 cups Greek yogurt, 3 eggs (2 yolks, 1 whole), 1/2 cup bloomed saffron water, 1/3 cup oil or butter, salt
  • Filling: 1.5 lbs chicken breast or thighs, 1 yellow onion, turmeric, salt, black pepper
  • Garnish: 1/2 cup dried barberries (zereshk), 1 tbsp butter, 1 tbsp sugar, slivered pistachios, slivered almonds

Steps

  1. Cook Chicken: Boil chicken with onion, turmeric, salt, and pepper until cooked, then shred into small pieces.
  2. Parboil Rice: Boil Basmati rice in salted water until soft on the outside but firm in the center, then drain.
  3. Mix Batter: Whisk yogurt, eggs, saffron water, oil, and salt in a large bowl. Stir the drained rice into the batter.
  4. Layer & Pack: Oil a non-stick pot. Press half the rice mixture firmly into the bottom, add the shredded chicken layer, and pack the remaining rice on top.
  5. Steam & Flip: Cover and cook on low heat for 50–60 minutes until a golden crust forms. Invert onto a plate and garnish with barberries sauteed in butter and sugar.
